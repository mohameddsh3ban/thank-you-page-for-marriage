# Sample photos

Every photo in this folder (`mm-01` … `mm-40`) is **AI-generated**. Mark and Mia are fictional
people: they do not exist, and no real person's photo or likeness was used as a reference.

- Generated with [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai) (Nano Banana image models), October 2026, from
  two invented character reference portraits so the couple stays consistent across every shot.
- Processed to webp: full size (max 1400px) plus a 480px thumbnail (`.t.webp`).
- They are placeholders for the template. Replace them with your own photos before you share your
  page (see "Use your own photos" in the README).
